"# A-E-T-H-E-R-M-O-O-R


## Core Premise
Aethermoor was broken ten years ago by an event called The Greying. The world is still wounded: Ashen creatures, corrupted relics, collapsing orders, and powerful institutions hiding the truth. You play as different heroes, each starting separately, carrying a personal wound tied to the Greying. Slowly, their individual stories point toward the same conspiracy.

## Characters & Lore

### Eryn Voss (Mage)
**Title:** The Exiled Scholar
Eryn was once a brilliant arcane scholar who believed the official explanation of the Greying was a lie. No one listened. She was exiled, disgraced, and reduced to surviving in Duskwall by doing card tricks and small magic for coin. Her central wound is vindication mixed with resentment. She was right, but being right cost her everything. When Ashen attack Duskwall, her magic returns violently, proving that the power inside her was never truly gone.

* **Personality:** Angry, intelligent, sharp-tongued. Haunted by exile and humiliation.
* **Gameplay Estimate:** High damage, low defense. Focuses on the "Raw Power" mechanic, unleashing massive uncontrolled magic (limited to three charges). Using it too much might affect her ending.

### Brennan Ashvane / Caden Ashford (Knight)
**Title:** The Oathbreaker
A former knight of the Iron Vow who deserted three years ago after something terrible happened—likely involving a burned village and dead civilians. He carries guilt like a sentence and keeps moving so no one can catch him. His central wound is shame, believing he failed his oath, though the order itself may be corrupt.

* **Personality:** Quiet, guarded, practical. Protective by instinct.
* **Gameplay Estimate:** High HP and strong defense. Utilizes a "Rage Mode" mechanic that boosts attack for several turns, showing the part of him that stops thinking and just fights.

### Solia Ren (Priest)
**Title:** The Faithless Healer
Solia was once connected to the Sacred Flame, but her title and license were revoked. Now she works in a refugee camp outside Valdenmere, healing people without permission because she cannot stop caring. Her central wound is spiritual doubt. She no longer knows if anyone is listening when she prays, but she prays anyway.

* **Personality:** Gentle but stubborn, compassionate, exhausted, quietly brave.
* **Gameplay Estimate:** Balanced healer. Uses "Divine Prayer" mechanic to heal and buff defense at the cost of limited faith points. Her faith can also be shaken by story events.

### Mira Cael (Ghost)
**Title:** The Ghost
"One minute for Lena. Then I go to work."
* **Gameplay Estimate:** High speed and stealth. Focuses on shadowy strikes and quick evasion.

### Sera Caldwell (Scout)
**Title:** The Eastern Scout
"I mentioned the wire. Watch the ground specifically."
* **Gameplay Estimate:** Ranged specialist. Utilizes focused shots to deal damage from afar.

## Main Factions
* **The Arcane Circle:** Magical scholars and authorities tied to Eryn's exile and the Greying cover-up.
* **The Iron Vow:** Knightly order tied to the Knight's past, now possibly corrupted under Lord Marshal Veyran.
* **The Sacred Flame:** Religious order tied to Solia's lost title and High Priest Aldran's extremist plans.
* **The Ashen Hand:** Cultists who worship or exploit the Greying.
* **The Architect:** A mysterious figure connected to the truth behind the Greying.

## Shared Themes
* Truth versus institutional lies
* Power versus restraint
* Faith after betrayal
* Guilt and redemption
* The danger of "cleansing" a wounded world
* Whether broken people can still save others
